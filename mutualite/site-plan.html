Mutualité

Mutualité Website Plan

Site Name

The name of the website is Mutualité.

The name represents the idea of mutual support, cooperation, unity, and collective responsibility within a community.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Mutualité website is to strengthen community engagement by providing a central platform where members can discover community initiatives, participate in activities, access mutual support resources, and stay connected with one another.

The website will promote collective initiatives and mutual funds that support community activities, cultural programs, social events, and projects that contribute to the well-being and development of members.

Mutualité will also encourage the preservation and growth of cultural values, traditions, heritage, and community identity by creating opportunities for members to share knowledge, celebrate their culture, and participate in meaningful community activities.

Scenarios

The following questions represent common reasons why visitors might use the Mutualité website:

  • How can I learn about upcoming Mutualité community events and activities?
  • How can I participate in community initiatives and cultural programs?
  • How does the Mutualité mutual fund support community members and activities?
  • Where can I find information about cultural traditions, heritage, and community values?
  • How can I contact Mutualité or become involved in its initiatives?
  • What activities are available for families, youth, and other members of the community?

Color Scheme

The Mutualité color scheme combines deep green, warm gold, ivory, and neutral colors. The palette communicates trust, community, growth, cultural heritage, and stability.

Primary — Deep Forest Green

#12372A

Used for the header, navigation, primary buttons, headings, and important sections.

Secondary — Evergreen

#1F5A43

Used for secondary sections, cards, hover states, and supporting interface elements.

Accent — Warm Gold

#D9A441

Used for highlights, icons, important links, borders, and calls to action.

Background — Soft Ivory

#FAF8F1

Used as the primary page background to provide a warm, welcoming appearance.

Surface — White

#FFFFFF

Used for cards, forms, content areas, and other surfaces that need visual separation.

Text — Near Black

#171717

Used for primary body text to provide strong readability and accessibility.

Visual Identity

The visual identity of Mutualité will communicate unity, belonging, growth, and cultural connection. Images will focus on community members, cultural activities, celebrations, collaboration, families, and community development initiatives.

The logo and visual elements will use the deep green and warm gold colors to create a recognizable identity across the website.

Wireframe

The Mutualité website wireframe will organize the main content into a clear hierarchy that allows visitors to quickly understand the organization's purpose and discover ways to participate.

Desktop Layout

  • Header with Mutualité logo and navigation
  • Hero section introducing the Mutualité mission
  • Community initiatives section
  • Upcoming events section
  • Mutual fund information section
  • Culture and heritage section
  • Community participation call-to-action
  • Footer with contact and social media information

Mobile Layout

  • Compact header with logo and hamburger navigation
  • Stacked hero content
  • Single-column information sections
  • Stacked initiative and event cards
  • Mobile-friendly contact form
  • Organized footer information
